rclone (Google OAuth app)

The Google Cloud app named "rclone" is a personal backup tool. It is used only by its owner, to sync the owner's own files between their computer and their own Google Drive with rclone.

It reads and writes files in the signed-in user's Google Drive only to perform that sync. The data goes directly between the user's computer and Google. It is not sent to any other server, not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising or analytics. Access tokens stay on the user's own computer, and access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
